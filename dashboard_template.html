Demo data

Read this first. Wise converts at the mid-market rate and charges a separate fee each time, so there is no buy/sell gap to arbitrage and any loop of conversions loses the fees. The only edge is timing: converting when the rate is unusually good for you. Rate forecasts here are probability ranges, and short-term FX moves are close to random.
